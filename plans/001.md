## Steps

### Step Group 1: Define Design System
**Files touched:** `DESIGN.md`
**What to do:** Create and fill in `DESIGN.md` with the full design system specifications. Do not leave any TBDs.

```markdown
# InterviewIQ Design System

## Brand & Tone
- **Name:** InterviewIQ
- **Tagline:** "Your AI Interview Coach"
- **Tone:** Confident, encouraging, precise.
- **Brand Identity:** Professional-yet-energetic.

## Color Palette
- **Theme:** Dark Theme
- **Primary:** `#6C63FF` (electric violet)
- **Secondary:** `#00D4AA` (mint green)
- **Background:** `#0F0F1A`
- **Surface:** `#1A1A2E`
- **Border:** `#2A2A3E`
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary:** `#A0A0C0`
- **Success:** `#00D4AA`
- **Warning:** `#FFB347`
- **Error:** `#FF6B6B`

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings Weight:** 700
- **Body Weight:** 400
- **Captions Weight:** 400
- **Scale:**
  - h1: 48px
  - h2: 36px
  - h3: 24px
  - h4: 20px
  - body: 16px
  - small: 14px
  - caption: 12px

## Spacing & Layout
- **Base Unit:** 4px
- **Scale:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96
- **Grid:** max-width 1200px, 24px gutter, 12-column.

## Components
- **Primary Button:** `#6C63FF` background, white text, 8px radius, hover darken 10%, transition 200ms.
- **Secondary Button:** Transparent background, `#6C63FF` border.
- **Ghost/Text Button:** Transparent background, no border, text-colored.
- **Card:** Surface background (`#1A1A2E`), border (`#2A2A3E`), 12px radius, 16px padding, subtle box-shadow.
- **Input:** Dark background, border (`#2A2A3E`), focus border (`#6C63FF`), 8px radius.
- **Score Ring:** Circular SVG progress ring with color coding:
  - `>80%`: Green (`#00D4AA`)
  - `60-80%`: Yellow (`#FFB347`)
  - `<60%`: Red (`#FF6B6B`)
- **Skill Badge:** Pill shape, background tinted by confidence level.
- **Progress Bar:** Thin, gradient fill.

## Key Screens
- **Resume Upload:** Centered card, drag-and-drop zone, file type hint, upload progress.
- **Company/Role Selection:** Grid of company cards with logos and persona description, role dropdown.
- **Live Interview:**
  - Left panel: Question + context
  - Right panel: Answer textarea + timer + progress indicator
  - Top: Session breadcrumbs
- **Final Report:**
  - Hero: Score ring
  - Tabs: Skills, Strengths, Weaknesses, Roadmap
  - Content: Per-skill accordion cards
```
**Verification:** Ensure `DESIGN.md` exists and contains no "TBD" placeholders.
